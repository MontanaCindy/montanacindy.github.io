Practitioner Research

Help us build something
actually useful.

We are building the Mindful Earth practitioner platform and we want to get it right. That means starting with your real experience — what is making your business harder than it should be, what you have tried, and what is missing. Five minutes of your time now shapes what we build.

Everyone who fills this in gets early access to the platform when it launches.

5 sections · about 5 minutes

About you

Quick context — so we understand who is telling us what.

Your name
Your email
What kind of practitioner are you?
How long have you been in practice?
Where are you based?
How do you currently work?

The hard parts

This is the most important section. Be honest — the more specific you are, the more useful it is.

What is the single biggest challenge in your business right now?
Which of these do you struggle with? (tick all that apply)
Anything else you would add to that list?

What you have already tried

Knowing what has not worked is just as useful as knowing what has.

What tools or platforms are you currently using to run your business?
What have you tried that has not worked?
What is one thing that has genuinely helped you?

What would actually help

We are building a platform for practitioners. We want to build what you actually need.

If we could solve one thing for you with this platform, what should it be?
Which of these would you use? (tick all that apply)
How much would you pay per month for a platform that genuinely solved your main problem?

Anything else

The thing we have not asked but you want to say.

Is there anything about your experience as a practitioner that you wish more people understood?
Would you be open to a 20-minute follow-up conversation about your experience?
How did you find Mindful Earth?

Thank you for your time.

Seriously. This kind of honesty is what helps us build something that actually works for practitioners rather than just another platform that looks good and solves the wrong problems. You will be the first to know when early access opens.

Received. Thank you.

We will be in touch when early access opens. In the meantime, join the Mindful Earth community on WhatsApp — a space for practitioners, seekers, and anyone building something meaningful in wellness.
