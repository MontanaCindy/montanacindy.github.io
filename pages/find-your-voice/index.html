Your Voice First.

How to use AI tools without losing the thing that makes your content worth reading.

You have probably noticed it. The caption that reads like it was written by committee. The newsletter that sounds polished but somehow empty. The retreat description that has all the right words but none of the feeling. This guide is about fixing that — not by using less AI, but by using it differently.

What's inside

A practical guide, not a lecture.

AI as a thinking partner

The difference between using AI as a ghostwriter and using it as a collaborator. One replaces your voice. The other amplifies it.

Prompts that actually work

AI prompt templates written specifically for wellness practitioners — for captions, retreat copy, emails, and the business side.

Authentic writing prompts

Prompts for you to write from first — before you open any AI tool. The material you find here is what makes everything that follows actually sound like you.

How to spot when AI has taken over

A simple checklist for reviewing your content before you post. If it sounds like it could have been written by anyone, it probably was.

Who this is for

For the practitioner who wants to use AI wisely.

You are not anti-AI. You are anti-sounding-like-everyone-else. You have spent years developing a point of view, a way of working, a voice — and you want to protect that while still working smarter.

This guide is for yoga teachers, retreat hosts, somatic practitioners, breathwork facilitators, wellness coaches — anyone whose work is personal, and whose marketing should reflect that.

"The point is not to use less AI. It is to use it better — with you in the driver's seat."

A note from Montana

Why I made this free.

I kept seeing practitioners I respect — people with real skill, real perspective, real things to say — sounding like everyone else online. Not because they had nothing to offer. Because the AI tools they were using to save time were quietly flattening everything that made their work recognisable.

I do not think the answer is to stop using AI. I think the answer is to use it in a way that keeps you in the lead. Your voice first. Every single time.

This guide is free because it should be in everyone's hands. Pass it on.

Montana Cindy

Founder, Mindful Earth
