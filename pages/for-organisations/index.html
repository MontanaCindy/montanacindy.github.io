The honest version

Most workplace wellness programmes do not work. This one is designed differently.

The generic yoga class on a Tuesday lunchtime. The mindfulness app subscription that three people actually use. The wellness day that lands in the diary and gets deprioritised when a meeting runs over. These things are not bad intentions — they are poor design. Wellness programming that works is not an add-on. It is embedded, it is evidenced, and it is delivered by people who have done the inner work themselves.

That is what we offer. Programmes designed around your team's actual needs, delivered by vetted practitioners who understand the nervous system, the body, and what sustained wellbeing actually requires.

What we offer organisations

Designed around your people. Delivered by practitioners.

Retreats

Team Retreats

One to three day offsites designed to genuinely reset your team — not just get them out of the office. Somatic practices, breathwork, nature connection, and structured reflection. We handle everything from venue to facilitation.

Workshops

Workplace Workshops

Half or full day workshops on nervous system regulation, breathwork, stress resilience, and sustainable performance. In-person or virtual. Built around your team's specific context — not a generic script.

Ongoing

Wellbeing Programmes

Quarterly or monthly programming — ongoing support that builds genuine resilience rather than delivering a one-off injection of wellness. Designed for organisations who take their people seriously as a long-term commitment.

Education

Eco & Wellness Education

The connection between personal wellbeing, community health, and environmental responsibility — explored through talks, workshops, and immersive experiences. Particularly relevant for purpose-driven organisations.

Who we work with.

We work best with organisations that genuinely care about their people — not ones looking to tick a wellbeing box on an HR checklist. B Corps, creative agencies, wellness brands, hospitality and venue groups, healthcare organisations, and purpose-driven businesses of all sizes. If your culture is aligned, we will find a way to work together that actually serves your team.
