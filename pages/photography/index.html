The approach

Photos that hold
the feeling.

The best retreat photos are not posed. They are the moment someone closes their eyes and exhales for the first time in weeks. The laughing over dinner. The quiet in the morning. The circle that forms without being asked.

I shoot retreats the way I experience them — as a practitioner who knows what it costs to create something like this, and who understands the atmosphere you have worked so hard to hold.
