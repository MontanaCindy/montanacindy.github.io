Work With Us

Building an
ecosystem together.

Mindful Earth is built by aligned humans, subcontracted and collaborative. If something here calls to you, we would love to hear from you.

How this works

Collaborative, project-based,
values-led.

These are subcontracted, project-based opportunities — not traditional employment. We work with aligned humans who care about the mission as much as the craft, and who want to contribute to building something genuinely supportive for wellness practitioners.

All roles are remote unless otherwise noted. Rates and scope are agreed per project. If you are excited about any of the roles below, scroll to the bottom for how to apply.

Open opportunities

Where we are looking for help.

Subcontracted · Ongoing

Social Media Manager

Strategy, content scheduling, community engagement, and growth across Instagram, Pinterest, and emerging platforms. You understand the wellness space and write in our voice.

Subcontracted · Project-based

Content Creator & Copywriter

Long-form articles, email sequences, landing page copy, and editorial work. You write with warmth and clarity, and understand both the business and the inner work behind wellness.

Subcontracted · Project-based

Graphic Designer

Brand-aligned design across digital assets, social, guides, and presentation materials. You understand editorial typography and quiet, intentional design.

Subcontracted · Phase 1 build

Web & Platform Developer

Full-stack development for the Mindful Earth platform (Phase 1 — Supabase, Next.js, Stripe Connect). Practitioner-facing tools and infrastructure.

Subcontracted · Per retreat or session

Wellness Practitioners

Yoga teachers, breathwork facilitators, somatic practitioners, sound healers, energy workers, movement teachers — we work with aligned facilitators for retreats and corporate wellness.

Subcontracted · Per retreat

Retreat & Event Coordinators

On-the-ground support during retreats — logistics, hosting, guest experience. You are calm under pressure and care about the details that make a container feel held.

Subcontracted · Per retreat or project

Retreat Photographer / Videographer

Documenting retreats and creating content for marketing. Particularly looking for photographers and videographers who can travel for retreats outside Montana’s coverage areas.

Subcontracted · Strategic advisory

Strategic Advisor / Specialist

Corporate wellness leads, business strategists, partnership specialists, marketing strategists. Open to senior practitioners or business minds who want to shape what we build.

What we look for

The kind of people we want to build with.

Mission alignment

You believe wellness should be accessible, and practitioners should be fairly compensated. Full stop.

Practitioner sensibility

You understand the work, not just the business. You have either lived this or worked closely with people who do.

Operational heart

You care about the details that make experiences feel held. Calm under pressure, generous with care.

Integrity over performance

You would rather be honest than impressive. You do good work whether or not anyone is watching.

How to apply

Send us a quick introduction.

If any of the roles above resonate, we would love to hear from you. Applications are straightforward — no portals, no forms, just an honest introduction.

Send to: connect@mindfulearth.eco

Include:

· The role(s) you are interested in
· A short CV or portfolio link
· A two-minute video introducing yourself — who you are, why this work calls to you, what you bring

The video matters more than the CV. We hire on alignment first, skills second. We do not have hard turnaround times on applications — we read everything that comes in and reply when there is a fit. Thank you for caring enough to apply.
