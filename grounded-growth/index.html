What's inside

Eight sections. One complete system.

Every section builds on the last. You cannot price before you vision. You cannot venue-source before you budget. The guide follows the actual order of decisions — so nothing falls through the gaps.

Section 01

Vision & Business Case

Who is this retreat for, what transformation does it create, and is it financially viable? The foundation everything else is built on.

Section 02

Budget & Pricing

Full budget template with every line item — including the ones people forget. Pricing calculator so you stop guessing and start knowing.

Section 03

Venue & Team

Venue sourcing checklist, the questions to ask before you sign anything, how to brief co-facilitators, and the local contacts that make a retreat feel alive.

Section 04

Food & Catering

How to brief a caterer, dietary requirements, the role food plays in the container, and why it matters more than people think.

Section 05

Participant Management

Intake forms, room breakdown and pricing by type, participant list with export — everything in one place to share with your team.

Section 06

Pre-Event & Day-Of

Welcome gifts, photographer briefing, run-of-show, group dynamics, and the week-before checklist that means nothing falls through.

Section 07

Legal & Safety

Insurance, waivers, refund policy, emergency protocols. The things nobody wants to deal with — until they need them.

Section 08

Post-Retreat Debrief

What to do in the 48 hours after. How to capture what worked. How to follow up with participants. How to make the next one better.
